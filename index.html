Profile

Townsend Bros Construction Co Ltd has been building forestry roads and infrastructure across Vancouver Island since 1966.

With nearly six decades of experience in the forestry sector, we have built a reputation for delivering high-quality road construction, earthworks, and infrastructure solutions in some of the most challenging terrain in the country.

Our team combines experienced operators with modern, well-maintained machinery to deliver projects safely, on time, and to the highest environmental standards. We work closely with forest managers, harvesting crews, and environmental consultants to ensure every project meets both operational and regulatory requirements.

From initial survey and design through to construction and ongoing maintenance, Townsend Bros provides a complete roading solution for the plantation forestry industry.

Services

Comprehensive forestry infrastructure solutions from planning through to completion.

Forestry Road Construction

New road construction through plantation forests including formation, and finishing to meet harvesting and transport requirements.

Earthworks & Site Development

Skid site formation, landing construction, log yard development, and bulk earthworks for forestry operations.

Bridge & Culvert Installation

Design and installation of stream crossings, bridges, culverts, and water management structures to environmental standards.

Quarry Operations

Rock extraction, crushing, and aggregate supply. On-site and mobile crushing to provide roading metal and fill material.

Road Maintenance & Rehabilitation

Ongoing road maintenance, grading, and rehabilitation of existing forestry road networks.

Drainage & Environmental

Comprehensive drainage design and installation, sediment control, and environmental management to protect waterways.
